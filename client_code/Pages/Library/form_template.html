Where do the stories come from?

Each tale is over a hundred years old and free for everyone: Robert Southey, the Brothers Grimm, Hans Christian Andersen, Joseph Jacobs, Aesop, St. Nicholas Magazine and folk tales from England, Norway and Russia. We retold them gently for little ones: the bears make friends, the giant is lonely rather than scary, the troll only needed a picnic, nobody eats the Gingerbread Man, and everybody shares the bread.
