Where do the stories come from?

Each classic tale is over a hundred years old and free for everyone: Robert Southey, the Brothers Grimm, Hans Christian Andersen, Joseph Jacobs, Aesop, St. Nicholas Magazine and folk tales from England, Norway, Russia and all over Europe. We retold them gently for little ones: the bears make friends, the giant is lonely rather than scary, the troll only needed a picnic, nobody eats the Gingerbread Man, and everybody shares the bread. The Lost Little Star, the Puddle Parade and the Hiccupy Dragon are brand-new stories, written for Little Hero Books.
