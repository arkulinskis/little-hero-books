Questions parents ask

Is it free?

Yes. Designing heroes, making books and downloading the PDFs are all free.

Do I need to upload a photo of my child?

No, never. You build the cartoon by picking features, so no photo of your child ever leaves your device.

What does the PDF look like?

Twelve square pages: a cover with your child, a “This book belongs to” page with your dedication, nine illustrated story pages and a happy “The End”. It prints nicely at home. For a photo-book printer, choose the print-ready version: 300 dpi pages in 8 × 8 in, 8.5 × 8.5 in or 20 × 20 cm, with the extra 3 mm bleed edge printers ask for. There is a colour-me-in outline version too.

Where do the stories come from?

They are fairy tales over a hundred years old and in the public domain (Robert Southey, the Brothers Grimm, Hans Christian Andersen, Joseph Jacobs, Aesop and traditional folk tales). We retold them gently for ages 3 to 6: the bears make friends, the giant is just lonely and the troll only needed a picnic.

Who can see my child’s name and cartoon?

Only you, when you’re signed in. If you make a family link for a book, people with that link can see that one book. You can turn the link off any time.

Can I try it before signing up?

Yes! Open the hero studio and read any story with your hero straight away. A free account lets you keep heroes, fill a bookshelf and download PDFs.
