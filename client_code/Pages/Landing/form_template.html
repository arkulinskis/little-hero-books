Questions parents ask

Is it free?

Yes. Designing heroes, making books and downloading the PDFs are all free.

Do I need to upload a photo of my child?

No, never. You build the cartoon by picking features, so no photo of your child ever leaves your device.

What does the PDF look like?

Twelve square pages: a cover with your child, a “This book belongs to” page with your dedication, nine illustrated story pages and a happy “The End”. It prints nicely at home. For a photo-book printer, choose the print-ready version: 300 dpi pages in 8 × 8 in, 8.5 × 8.5 in or 20 × 20 cm, with the extra 3 mm bleed edge printers ask for. There is a colour-me-in outline version too.

Where do the stories come from?

Sixteen are over a hundred years old and in the public domain. Fifteen are fairy tales and fables (Robert Southey, the Brothers Grimm, Hans Christian Andersen, Joseph Jacobs, Aesop and traditional folk tales) that we retold gently for ages 3 to 6: the bears make friends, the giant is just lonely and the troll only needed a picnic. The sixteenth is The Night Before Christmas, printed exactly as the poem first appeared in 1823, with your child listening to it on Christmas Eve. Three (the Lost Little Star, the Puddle Parade and the Hiccupy Dragon) are brand-new stories written for Little Hero Books.

Can I write my own story?

Yes. Open “Write” to start from a blank page, or take any story from the library and change the words, the pictures and the number of pages. Your hero steps into every picture you choose, and your stories stay private to you. Make one into a book to read it aloud, print it or share it with family.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes, and it can’t arrive late. On your book’s page press Gift it, pick the wrapping paper, who it’s from and the day it opens (Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, any day, or straight away). The book’s family link then shows a wrapped present that counts the sleeps, and on the day your child taps the bow to unwrap it. Save the gift card with its QR code to print and put under the tree. Grown-ups can peek inside first to check the spelling and the likeness.

Who can see my child’s name and cartoon?

Only you, when you’re signed in. If you make a family link for a book, people with that link can see that one book. You can turn the link off any time.

Can I try it before signing up?

Yes! Open the hero studio and read any story with your hero straight away. A free account lets you keep heroes, fill a bookshelf and download PDFs.
